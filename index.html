Jason Pratt is an accomplished acoustic guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter whose music blends heartfelt authenticity with decades of stage experience. A proud Berklee College of Music alumnus, Jason has spent over 30 years performing across a wide variety of venues, connecting with audiences through both his soulful originals and a rich selection of “deep cut” covers — the timeless songs people know and love, but rarely hear live. With a warm, expressive voice and dynamic guitar style, Jason brings new life to every performance, creating an intimate and memorable musical experience. His original compositions showcase his artistry and storytelling, while his taste in covers reveals a deep appreciation for the craft and legacy of great songwriting. Whether playing a cozy acoustic set or commanding a festival stage, Jason Pratt delivers music that resonates — familiar yet fresh, rooted yet inspired.
